about=Implements the "grid method" (坵塊法) slope analysis required by Taiwan's Soil and Water Conservation Technical Regulations. Draw a rectangular analysis frame on the map (move / rotate / resize it interactively), choose a 10 m or 25 m cell, and the plugin fills the frame with square cells starting from the top-left corner, counts intersections between each cell edge and the contour lines, and computes the mean slope S = nπΔh/(8L)×100 and inclination θ for every cell. Slope grades 1–7 (Art. 23) and cell aspect (Art. 26) are written to attribute fields. Terrain input can be contour lines or a DEM (contours are generated automatically). The plugin styles and labels the results like a conventional hand-drawn grid slope map, builds a print layout (legend, north arrow, scale bar, statistics table, formula notes), and saves the whole project into a new GeoPackage plus a new .qgz project file. 依水土保持技術規範坵塊法計算坡度、分級、坡向並自動產製坵塊圖、版面與 GeoPackage 專案。
